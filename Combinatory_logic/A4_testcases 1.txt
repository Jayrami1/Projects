General instructions:
- Use your own AST and constructors for lam / comb.
- Pipeline for each test:
    lam → translate → comb → wnf(c, [])
- Print final result using comb_to_string (or equivalent).
- Output format:
    variables: x, y, z, f, etc.
    application: (a b)
